Tabs

A tab for everything your group talks about

Everything updates live. When someone adds something, everyone else sees it right away.

The screenshots show the app in Turkish; it works in English too.

  1. 01

    List

    Someone claims each item, so nobody brings three of the same thing and nothing gets forgotten.

  2. 02

    Expenses

    Enter what you spent. The app works out who owes whom, down to the cent.

  3. 03

    Polls

    Which day, what time, where to eat. Let the group vote instead of arguing.

  4. 04

    Chat and notes

    Ticket times and meeting points don't get buried under hundreds of messages.

  5. 05

    Memories

    Everyone's photos end up in one shared album.

  6. 06

    Trip report

    When the trip ends you get a summary. Who was the most generous, who talked the most.

  7. 07

    Profile and badges

    Your trip history adds up, and new places unlock badges.
