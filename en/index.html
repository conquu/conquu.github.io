Studio

ConquApp is an independent mobile app studio. We make a small number of apps and support each one for the long run.

GezPlan grew out of the mess friend groups run into before a trip. The plan got lost in the group chat, the bill ended up on a napkin, and three people brought the same thing. The app pulls all of that onto one screen and has been updated regularly since launch.
